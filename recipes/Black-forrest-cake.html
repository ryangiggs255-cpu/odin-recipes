Black Forrest Cake

Description

Black Forest cake is a German classic — layers of rich chocolate sponge soaked in cherry liqueur,

stacked with whipped cream and a tart cherry filling, then finished with more whipped

cream, chocolate shavings, and fresh cherries on top. The combination of bitter dark chocolate,

sweet cream, and slightly sharp cherries is what makes it distinctive — it's rich but never one-note.

It's a showpiece cake, usually reserved for celebrations, and the cross-section cut (dark sponge,

white cream, red cherry filling in alternating layers) is as much the point as the taste.

Ingredients

For the chocolate sponge:

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup hot coffee or hot water
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

For the filling and assembly:

  • 2 cans (400g each) pitted dark cherries, drained, juice reserved
  • 1/4 cup kirsch (cherry liqueur) or cherry juice, for soaking
  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Chocolate shavings or curls, for decoration
  • Fresh cherries, for garnish

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease and line two round cake pans.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Add the sugar, eggs, buttermilk, oil, and vanilla. Mix until smooth.
  4. Slowly stir in the hot coffee or water — the batter will be thin, that's expected.
  5. Divide the batter between the two pans and bake for 30–35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Let the cakes cool completely, then slice each one horizontally in half to make four layers total.
  7. Brush each sponge layer with the kirsch or reserved cherry juice to soak in the flavor.
  8. Whip the cream with powdered sugar and vanilla until stiff peaks form.
  9. Place one sponge layer on a serving plate, spread whipped cream over it, then scatter a layer of cherries on top.
  10. Repeat with the remaining layers, finishing with a generous layer of whipped cream on top.
  11. Cover the sides with whipped cream, then press chocolate shavings around the edge.
  12. Decorate the top with more cream swirls, chocolate shavings, and fresh cherries.
  13. Chill for at least an hour before slicing, so the layers set.

Banana Bread

Description

Banana bread is a moist, dense quick bread built around overripe bananas — the browner and

spottier, the better, since that's when they're sweetest and easiest to mash. Unlike yeast breads,

it doesn't need proofing or kneading; baking soda does the lifting, which makes it a forgiving

bake even for beginners. The batter comes together in one bowl in minutes, and the result is a

loaf with a golden, slightly cracked top and a soft, banana-forward crumb — equally good as a

breakfast slice, a snack, or dessert with a pat of butter while it's still warm.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease a loaf pan.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, mash the bananas with a fork until mostly smooth.
  3. Stir the melted butter into the mashed banana.
  4. Mix in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
  5. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir in.
  6. Add the flour and mix just until combined — don't overmix, or the bread turns dense and tough.
  7. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
  8. Bake for 50–60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  9. Let it cool in the pan for about 10 minutes, then turn it out onto a rack to cool completely before slicing.
